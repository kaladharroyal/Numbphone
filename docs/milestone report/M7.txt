1. OBJECTIVES & DELIVERABLES ACHIEVED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Adaptive Exit Friction Domain Engine (`core:domain:friction`):
  * `CalculateExitFrictionUseCase`: Implements exact exponential progression:
    - Attempt 1 (0 prior) -> 5 seconds
    - Attempt 2 (1 prior) -> 30 seconds
    - Attempt 3 (2 prior) -> 120 seconds (2 minutes)
    - Attempt 4+ (3+ prior) -> 600 seconds (10 minutes)
  * `PrepareExitAttemptUseCase`: Calculates current attempt sequence, remaining session minutes, and required friction delay.
  * `CompleteExitAttemptUseCase`: Unlocks and cancels session when friction delay is completed, logging successful exit to `ExitAttemptRepository`.
  * `AbortExitAttemptUseCase`: Increments bypass attempt counter and logs aborted exit attempt when the user chooses to continue focusing.
  * `RecordIntentReflectionUseCase`: Records "Why are you opening this?" survey choices (Important/Urgent, Just checking, I'm bored, Someone sent me something, Other) to `BlockedAttemptRepository`.

- Exit Friction UI & Dialog (`core:ui:components`):
  * `ExitFrictionDialog`:
    - Shows original intention card: *"Your Goal: [Goal Title]"* with remaining time.
    - Displays progressive attempt badge: *"EXIT ATTEMPT #[N]"*.
    - Non-shaming, mindful message: *"You chose a focus session. Take a moment before leaving."*
    - Live countdown timer with *"Wait [X] seconds"* lock.
    - Disabled *"WAITING (Xs)"* button until countdown finishes, then transforms into an active *"EXIT ANYWAY"* button.
    - High-contrast *"CONTINUE FOCUS"* button to easily resume.
  * `IntentReflectionDialog`:
    - Clean monochrome modal for intentional reflection.
    - Radio choices for intent reason + custom reason input field.

- Feature Integrations (`feature:focussession` & `feature:homescreen`):
  * `FocusSessionViewModel` & `FocusSessionScreen`: Intercepts "LEAVE FOCUS SESSION" taps during `STRICT` and `DEEP_FOCUS` modes to trigger `ExitFrictionDialog`.
  * `HomeViewModel` & `HomeScreen`: Triggers `IntentReflectionDialog` during Light mode launches or inspection of restricted apps, logging user intent to Room.
